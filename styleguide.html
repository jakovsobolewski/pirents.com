Accessibility checklist

  • ${check}Focus ring on every interactive element: 3 px Paper gap + 3 px Brand Red. Press Tab to see it.
  • ${check}44 × 44 px minimum hit area. Buttons are 44 px, inputs 48 px.
  • ${check}Gold is never used as text. Brand Red passes 4.5:1 on white; links use Red 700.
  • ${check}Status always has an icon or word, never colour alone.
  • ${check}Listing cards expose one link with the full accessible name; the favourite button is separate.
  • ${check}Reduced motion disables transitions and animations.
  • ${check}Skip link, landmarks, labelled form fields, live regions for toasts and results.
