How it works

Rent from a local. List for a traveller.

Pirents connects people who own mobility and transport gear with people who need it for a few days. Here's exactly what happens on both sides.

For renters

From search to handover

1

Search your destination

Enter a city and dates. Filter by category, features like folding or delivery, and structured specs such as seat width or range.

2

Check the details

Every listing shows weight, dimensions, limits, the owner's rules and real reviews. Message the owner if anything is unclear.

3

Book with a clear total

The price, the 10% service fee and the refundable deposit are all shown before you commit. Owners confirm within 24 hours, or instantly.

4

Meet and go

Get the item delivered, handed over at the airport or pick it up. A quick check together, then it's yours for the trip.

Common questions

When am I charged?${chevron}

For "request to book" listings, only once the owner confirms. For instant-book listings, at the time of booking. Deposits are held from pickup and released within 3 days of return.

What if the item is faulty or not as described?${chevron}

Tell us within 24 hours of pickup with a photo. We refund in full and help you find an alternative nearby.

Can someone else drive the car or use the chair?${chevron}

Yes, as long as the named renter is present at handover. For cars, additional drivers must be added before pickup so they're covered by the insurance.

Do you rent medical equipment?${chevron}

We list mobility aids that are safe for general use: wheelchairs, scooters, rollators, ramps, shower chairs and similar. We do not list prescription or clinical devices such as oxygen equipment or hospital beds.

What does the owner see about me?${chevron}

Your first name, verified badge, reviews from previous owners and your message. Your full name and phone number are shared once a booking is confirmed.

How do cancellations work?${chevron}

Free cancellation until 48 hours before pickup. After that, the first day's rental is kept. Owners who cancel a confirmed booking are penalised in search ranking.
