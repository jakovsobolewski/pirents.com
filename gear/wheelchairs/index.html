Questions about renting here

Where can I rent a wheelchair with Pirents?

In Lisbon, Barcelona, Amsterdam, Athens, Greece, Split, Rome, Vienna, with owners joining in more cities each month.

What should I look for in a rented wheelchair?

A lightweight folding chair (under 12 kg) is easiest for taxis and cobbles when someone is pushing; a power chair is better for long museum days.

Is delivery or airport handover available?

Both, depending on the owner. Every listing shows its handover options, and each city has an airport handover page.
