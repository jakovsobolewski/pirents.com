Questions before you book

Can I rent a wheelchair or mobility scooter for just one day?${chevron}

Yes. Most Pirents listings have a one-day minimum. Owners set their own minimum and it is shown on every listing before you book.

Do owners deliver to my hotel or meet me at the airport?${chevron}

Many do. Listings show delivery, airport handover, station meet-up or pick-up from the owner's address. Filter by "Delivery available" or "Airport handover" on the browse page.

What does the price include?${chevron}

The daily price set by the owner plus a 10% Pirents service fee, shown before you commit. A refundable deposit is held from pick-up to return. Every rental includes up to €3,000 damage cover.

Who am I renting from?${chevron}

A verified local owner with a name, reviews and a typical response time. You message them directly before and during the rental.

Which cities is Pirents live in?${chevron}

Lisbon, Barcelona, Amsterdam, Athens (Greece), Split, Rome and Vienna, with owners joining in more cities every month. If your destination is empty, tell us what you need and we will find it.
