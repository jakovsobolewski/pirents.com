# SEO changelog

What was implemented on the site from `docs/seo-competitor-analysis.md`, and what is still open.

## Implemented (29 September 2026)

### Technical foundations (report P0)
- `robots.txt` (dashboard and style guide disallowed) and `sitemap.xml`, regenerated by the build script and listing only indexable URLs.
- Unique title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter tags on every page. Dashboard and style guide are `noindex`.
- Version strings on CSS and JS links so browsers never serve stale assets.
- Structured data: Organization and WebSite with SearchAction plus FAQPage on the home page; FAQPage and HowTo on how-it-works; HowTo on list-your-item; ItemList on search results; Product with Offer and AggregateRating, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage on listings.

### Pre-rendered landing pages (report P0 item 1 and P2 item 1 to 2)
`node scripts/build-pages.js` generates real HTML from the seed data, with the listing grid, local copy, handover section, FAQ, cross-links and JSON-LD in the page:

| Pattern | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `rentals/{city}/` | 7 | City hub: category tiles with counts and from-prices, popular listings, handover, transit, FAQ |
| `rentals/{city}/{category}/` | 56 | The money pages. Pages with no listings are `noindex, follow` and carry a request CTA |
| `rentals/{city}/airport/` | 7 | Airport handover page with `Airport` schema; noindex where no owner offers it |
| `gear/{category}/` | 8 | Category hub linking to every city |

Athens is always "Athens, Greece" (slug `athens-greece`) to avoid the Athens, Georgia SERP pollution the report found.

### On-page changes (report P1)
- Home: keyword-first title, keyword-bearing section headings, city strip and category tiles link to the static pages, airport handover strip, five-question FAQ with schema.
- Browse: canonical points to the static city or category page when only city and category are set; `noindex, follow` when dates or filters are applied.
- Listing: title and description templates, breadcrumb Home > City > Category > Listing, explicit handover section, templated three-question FAQ with schema, "More {category} in {city}" and "Other gear from {owner}" blocks.
- How it works: new title, FAQPage and HowTo schema.
- List your item: keyword H1 "Rent out your wheelchair, scooter or bike to travellers", owner benefits in the lead, HowTo schema.

## Still open
- Real photos on listings (Product rich results require an image). Listing pages currently show category tiles.
- The seven city accessibility guides and the evergreen explainers (report section 6). No `guides/` pages exist yet.
- Owner-side category pages (`rent-out/{category}`).
- Trust numbers (reviews, listings, cities) on the home page once real.
- The "Prototype" footer line must be removed before launch.
- Listing URLs are still `listing.html?id=`; pretty `listings/{id}-{slug}` URLs need a server or a pre-render step per listing.
- Cruise-port pages and neighbourhood pages (report P3).

## How to rebuild
After changing `assets/js/data.js` or the copy in `scripts/build-pages.js`:

```bash
node scripts/build-pages.js
```
