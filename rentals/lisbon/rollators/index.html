Can I take a rollator on public transport in Lisbon?

Metro: lifts at most stations. Tram 15E to Belém is accessible; Tram 28 is not. Buses have ramps. The Santa Justa lift and the Baixa-Chiado escalators save the steepest climbs. A carbon rollator under 7 kg lifts easily into a taxi; a seat and a bag make queues and cafés far more comfortable.
