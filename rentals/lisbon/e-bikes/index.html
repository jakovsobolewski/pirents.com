Getting around Lisbon

Steep hills in Alfama, Bairro Alto and Graça; flat along the river and in Baixa. Calçada is slippery when wet.

In hilly cities choose an e-bike with a mid-drive motor and at least 400 Wh. Cargo bikes are the easiest way to move two children and a beach bag.

Can I get a e-bike handed over at Lisbon Humberto Delgado Airport?

Owners marked "Airport handover" meet you at LIS arrivals. See Lisbon listings with airport handover.

Can I take a bike on public transport in Lisbon?

Metro: lifts at most stations. Tram 15E to Belém is accessible; Tram 28 is not. Buses have ramps. The Santa Justa lift and the Baixa-Chiado escalators save the steepest climbs.
