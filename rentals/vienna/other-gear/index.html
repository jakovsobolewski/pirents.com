Questions about renting here

Can I rent a mobility aid in Vienna for one day?

Yes. Most Pirents owners in Vienna rent by the day with a one-day minimum; the minimum is shown on each listing. Competing hire companies usually require three days.

Do owners deliver to hotels in Vienna?

Many do. Listings marked "Delivery available" bring the mobility aid to your hotel or apartment. Others hand over at Vienna International Airport (VIE), at Wien Hauptbahnhof or at the owner's address.

Can I get a mobility aid handed over at Vienna International Airport?

Owners marked "Airport handover" meet you at VIE arrivals. See Vienna listings with airport handover.

What other mobility aids can I rent in Vienna?

U-Bahn: lifts at every station. Low-floor ULF trams and all buses are accessible. CAT and S7 trains run from the airport to the centre. A folding suitcase ramp solves the two steps into most old-town apartments; check the length against the step height.
