Can I rent a mobility scooter in Barcelona for one day?

Yes. Most Pirents owners in Barcelona rent by the day with a one-day minimum; the minimum is shown on each listing. Competing hire companies usually require three days.

Do owners deliver to hotels in Barcelona?

Many do. Listings marked "Delivery available" bring the mobility scooter to your hotel or apartment. Others hand over at Barcelona El Prat Airport (BCN), at Sants station or at the owner's address.
