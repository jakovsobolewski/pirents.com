How does airport handover work at ATH?

After booking you agree a time with the owner. They wait in the arrivals hall with the gear, do a two-minute handover and you leave with it. Return handover at departures works the same way.

Can I also get it delivered to my hotel instead?

Yes. Most owners who do airport handover also deliver in Athens, Greece. Pick whichever suits your arrival time.

How do I get from ATH to the centre with a scooter or wheelchair?

Metro: lifts at all stations. Trams and newer buses are accessible. The metro runs directly to Athens International Airport and to Piraeus port.
