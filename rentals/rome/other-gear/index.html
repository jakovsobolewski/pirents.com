Questions about renting here

Can I rent a mobility aid in Rome for one day?

Yes. Most Pirents owners in Rome rent by the day with a one-day minimum; the minimum is shown on each listing. Competing hire companies usually require three days.

Do owners deliver to hotels in Rome?

Many do. Listings marked "Delivery available" bring the mobility aid to your hotel or apartment. Others hand over at Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO), at Termini station or at the owner's address.

Can I get a mobility aid handed over at Rome Fiumicino Airport?

Owners marked "Airport handover" meet you at FCO arrivals. See Rome listings with airport handover.

What other mobility aids can I rent in Rome?

Metro A and B are partly accessible (check the station). Buses have ramps. The Leonardo Express train connects Fiumicino Airport and Termini in 32 minutes. A folding suitcase ramp solves the two steps into most old-town apartments; check the length against the step height.
