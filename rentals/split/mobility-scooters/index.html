Yes. Most Pirents owners in Split rent by the day with a one-day minimum; the minimum is shown on each listing. Competing hire companies usually require three days.

Do owners deliver to hotels in Split?

Many do. Listings marked "Delivery available" bring the mobility scooter to your hotel or apartment. Others hand over at Split Airport (SPU), at Split bus and train station by the port or at the owner's address.

Are mobility scooters allowed on public transport in Split?

The centre is walkable and flat along the Riva. Local buses are partly accessible. Ferries take mobility gear on the vehicle deck; ask the owner about the ferry ramp.
