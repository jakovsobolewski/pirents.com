Questions about renting here

Can I rent a beach wheelchair in Split for one day?

Yes. Most Pirents owners in Split rent by the day with a one-day minimum; the minimum is shown on each listing. Competing hire companies usually require three days.

Do owners deliver to hotels in Split?

Many do. Listings marked "Delivery available" bring the beach wheelchair to your hotel or apartment. Others hand over at Split Airport (SPU), at Split bus and train station by the port or at the owner's address.

Can I get a beach wheelchair handed over at Split Airport?

Owners marked "Airport handover" meet you at SPU arrivals. See Split listings with airport handover.

Which beaches in Split are accessible?

The centre is walkable and flat along the Riva. Local buses are partly accessible. Ferries take mobility gear on the vehicle deck; ask the owner about the ferry ramp. Beach chairs need a companion to push in soft sand; many owners deliver straight to the beach and collect afterwards.

How much does it cost to rent a beach wheelchair in Split?

From €22 per day plus a 10% service fee, with a refundable deposit held from pick-up to return. The full total is shown before you book.
