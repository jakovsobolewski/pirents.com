Can I rent a mobility scooter in Amsterdam for one day?

Yes. Most Pirents owners in Amsterdam rent by the day with a one-day minimum; the minimum is shown on each listing. Competing hire companies usually require three days.

Do owners deliver to hotels in Amsterdam?

Many do. Listings marked "Delivery available" bring the mobility scooter to your hotel or apartment. Others hand over at Amsterdam Schiphol Airport (AMS), at Amsterdam Centraal or at the owner's address.

Can I get a mobility scooter handed over at Amsterdam Schiphol Airport?

Owners marked "Airport handover" meet you at AMS arrivals. See Amsterdam listings with airport handover.

Are mobility scooters allowed on public transport in Amsterdam?

Metro and low-floor trams (lines with the newer stock) are accessible; older trams are not. Mobility scooters and e-bikes use the cycle paths. Direct train from Schiphol to Centraal.
